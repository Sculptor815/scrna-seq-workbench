# Choose sample-aware quality filters and retain an exclusion ledger

Learning draft. Code behavior is tied to the source snapshot below; scientific review is pending.

## Principle

Total counts measure captured signal; detected genes measure observed complexity; mitochondrial fraction measures the share assigned to identified mitochondrial genes. Each can reflect both biology and technical damage. Low-RNA populations may be real, and a high mitochondrial fraction is not a universal diagnosis of a dead cell.

## Decision sequence

1. Establish raw-count and gene-symbol provenance. Inspect distributions per sample or physical capture before filtering.
2. Start with `--inspect-only`. Read cell metrics and threshold suggestions; ask whether samples differ systematically.
3. Choose thresholds from assay context, distributions and population preservation. Record units, rationale and an alternative plausible setting for sensitivity analysis.
4. Run filters in a fresh directory. Compare retained cells by sample and known or suspected population; inspect the first exclusion reason per cell.
5. Investigate doublets when relevant using physical captures. Assess ambient RNA separately; this plugin does not perform ambient-RNA correction.

## Actual plugin behavior

Metrics use raw counts. Human and mouse symbol patterns identify mitochondrial, ribosomal and hemoglobin genes. No matching symbols can produce a zero percentage with a warning; that is not evidence of zero mitochondrial RNA. An active mitochondrial or hemoglobin filter with no matching genes is rejected.

Suggested thresholds use median and MAD-based calculations and are not automatically applied. Lower bounds are inclusive; upper bounds are exclusive. The cell ledger records the first failed criterion, not every failure. Gene filtering follows cell filtering. Scrublet is optional, runs per capture and requires at least 30 eligible cells in a capture; its removal option is explicit. These are implementation constraints, not biological guarantees.

## Outputs and interpretation

Read `cell_qc.csv`, `gene_qc.csv`, `qc_by_sample.png` and `report.json`. Inspect-only mode does not create a filtered matrix. The final counts layer contains retained cells and genes, while the downloaded input remains the independent source of excluded material.

## Self-check

**Question:** Should one fixed mitochondrial cutoff be copied from blood to every tissue?

**Suggested answer:** No. Evaluate tissue, preparation and per-sample distributions, then check which populations are lost under plausible alternatives.
