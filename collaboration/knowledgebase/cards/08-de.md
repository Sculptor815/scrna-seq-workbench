# Estimate condition differences at the donor level

Learning draft. Scientific review is pending.

## Principle

Cells within a donor share biology and processing. Treating them as independent people can understate uncertainty. Pseudobulk sums raw counts within donor, condition and cell type, then models biological groups. A negative-binomial count model accounts for variability beyond a simple Poisson assumption; its design determines which contrast is estimated.

## Decision sequence

1. Confirm reviewed labels, raw counts and real donor/condition mappings. Inspect confounding before fitting.
2. Choose paired analysis only when the same donor supplies both conditions. Unpaired analysis needs distinct donors across groups.
3. Inspect cells per donor-condition-type group. Exclude insufficient groups transparently; an absent population is not an observed zero-expression sample.
4. Fit the supported design and check effect direction, effect magnitude, dispersion/outlier handling and uncertainty together.
5. Interpret adjusted p-values within their stated testing family. Keep undefined adjusted p-values visible and inspect why they are undefined.
6. Check sensitivity to plausible QC/annotation choices. Report association unless the design supports stronger inference.

## Actual plugin behavior

The default group minimum is 20 cells. The minimum biological replication setting cannot be below 3: at least 3 donors per condition for unpaired analysis, or 3 complete pairs for paired analysis. These are software eligibility floors, not a power calculation or universal sufficiency rule. Two young versus three old donors fails this runner's unpaired gate.

The supported formulas are `~condition` and `~donor+condition`; arbitrary covariates are not supported. PyDESeq2 fits counts with its configured outlier handling. This runner does not explicitly apply log-fold-change shrinkage. Adjusted p-values are produced per cell type, not across the entire study. Unknown/unassigned populations are excluded. If no eligible fit remains, the run is blocked, not a negative biological result.

## Self-check

**Question:** Can five random cell subsets from one donor stand in for five donors?

**Suggested answer:** No. They do not create independent biological replication. Request additional samples or restrict the claim.
