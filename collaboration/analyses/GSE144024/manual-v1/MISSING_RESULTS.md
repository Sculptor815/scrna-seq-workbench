## Smallest useful next delivery

1. The final cluster-to-label table and per-cell annotation export, linked to the exact h5ad and clustering version.
2. The executed QC/scVI/graph/clustering configuration, retention log and cell-cycle/doublet diagnostics.
3. The completed per-dataset DE tables and quantitative marker summaries.
4. Any already-generated parameter comparison gallery and the user's chosen settings.

The original h5ad and model can remain in external storage: a stable location and checksum can support provenance without committing large raw data to Git. Keep the supplied manual result unchanged while adding these materials.
