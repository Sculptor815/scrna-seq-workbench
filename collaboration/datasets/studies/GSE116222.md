## Source and paper

- [GEO GSE116222](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE116222)
- Parikh et al. (2019), *Colonic epithelial cell diversity in health and
  inflammatory bowel disease*.
- [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-0992-y) |
  [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30814735/)
- GEO describes 10x experiments with healthy colon and inflamed / adjacent
  non-inflamed ulcerative colitis biopsies; nine sample records are listed.
  Sample records must be mapped to donors and pairing before a comparison.

comparison. Confirm which samples and conditions are retained. The GEO filename

## Proposed biological question

Can the agent distinguish epithelial identities and describe goblet-cell/barrier
expression patterns with appropriate uncertainty? If original counts, donors
and paired biopsies can be recovered, separately assess which condition question
is supported by the design and current pseudobulk implementation.
