The [HPA archive](https://www.proteinatlas.org/download/tsv/rna_single_cell_read_count.zip)
contains a `retina/` tissue export; HPA lists 7,222 cells. The files have not been
downloaded or hash-verified here and may cover only a subset of the original study.

## Proposed biological question

Can the agent identify major retinal cell populations and explain which cell
types express a researcher-specified gene panel? A regional comparison requires
verified region and donor metadata. Gene expression or disease-associated gene
enrichment alone does not establish a causal role in macular degeneration.

## Checks before selection

- Confirm counts, ordered gene/cell identities and metadata joins.
- Check the retained donor and region composition for the chosen copy.
- Specify a retina-matched marker panel, rare-cell targets and label review.
- Separate published labels and coordinates from annotation-test inputs.
- Define descriptive outputs first. Disease enrichment or circuit connectivity
  inference is beyond the current numerical runner's supported scope.
- Read the relevant paper figures and Methods; record hashes and source terms.
