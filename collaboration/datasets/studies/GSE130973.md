# GSE130973 - Human skin aging

**Status:** proposed for collaborator selection. GEO/HPA metadata and publication
identity checked; matrices and detailed Methods not yet reviewed locally.

## Source and paper

- [GEO GSE130973](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE130973)
- Sole-Boldo et al. (2020), *Single-cell transcriptomes of the human skin reveal
  age-related loss of fibroblast priming*.
- [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-020-0922-4) |
  [Open full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7181753/)
- GEO describes five donors: two younger and three older. The small and
  unbalanced donor groups limit age-associated inference even with many cells.

## Proposed biological question

Can the agent distinguish dermal fibroblast states and propose a defensible
analysis of age-associated expression? The agent should first establish whether
the available donors, covariates and reviewed labels support the requested
comparison, and explain uncertainty from the small donor sample.

## Checks before selection

- Inspect the original sample metadata and potential confounders.
- Validate raw/filtered count provenance and file alignment.
- Establish barcode-to-donor mapping; never treat cells as donor replicates.
- Use a skin-specific marker panel and review fibroblast subtypes.
- Restrict results to supported associations; cross-sectional RNA data alone
  do not establish an aging mechanism or cell-cell communication.
- Check the runner's design requirements before promising DE; record a
  limitation when the requested model or sample design is unsupported.
- Select source figures and Methods; record hashes, resource use and terms.
