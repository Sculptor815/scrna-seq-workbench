The [download manifest](../provenance/GSE115469-hpa-v25.1.json) records hashes and
sizes without machine-specific paths. Local validation found 20,162 unique gene
IDs, 6,404 unique cell IDs, nonnegative integer values, 10,244,822 nonzero entries
and a count sum of 32,749,593. Matrix cell order matches `cell_data.tsv`.

GEO sample metadata describes the submitted expression values as **log2CPM**
([sample record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM3178782&targ=self&form=text&view=full)).
Do not round those normalized values or relabel them as raw UMI counts.

## Proposed biological question

Which liver macrophage expression patterns can be supported by this matrix,
and how confidently can they be distinguished from other immune populations?
Start with descriptive expression evidence and uncertainty in cell identity.
Validate any resident/recruited or functional interpretation with additional
biological evidence rather than deriving lineage or causality from clustering.
